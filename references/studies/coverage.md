# Navigation coverage ledger

Snapshot: 29 September 2026. **Research is incomplete.** 477 distinct discovered URLs include anchors, external destinations, account entry points, and some duplicate page bodies reached through different URLs. This is not a count of unique templates or completed studies.

A fetch, browser capture, and analyst review are different evidence levels. Limited visual reviews cover desktop opening composition and heading structure, not every section, mobile layout, or interaction. Source captures remain research working files; this public library distributes original notes and code, not reference assets.

## Known gaps

- Perplexity presents an access challenge; Ramp returned machine-readable text instead of the visual site.
- Cloudflare menu discovery is incomplete; Retool Audience was not expanded. Other observed desktop menu sets are not independently certified complete.
- External ecosystem links are inventoried but mostly not browser-captured; account entry points were not authenticated.
- HTTP errors, rate limits, empty shells, and navigation timeouts are not design evidence. No evasion or authenticated access was attempted.
- Hundreds of captures remain unreviewed. All-site, all-main-navigation, full-page/mobile/motion coverage has **not** been achieved.
